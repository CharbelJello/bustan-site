The orchard, on record

Bustan (Arabic for orchard) is farm management for apple farms in Lebanon. It replaces word of mouth with dated records: which blocks were sprayed and when they can be picked again, what each block yields, when each block gets water, what is owed to each foreman, and how many boxes of each variety are left.

Spray waiting periods

Every treatment records its chemicals, and Bustan refuses a harvest until the waiting period has passed.

Harvests and stock

Boxes per block, per variety and per season, sales to buyers, losses, and what is left in storage.

Water turns

Which block gets water from which source, and when, week by week.

Crews and pay

Daily attendance by foreman, wages, and how much is still owed.

Your farm's data stays on your farm

Bustan runs on a small server at the farm (a Raspberry Pi). The farm's records live on that server, not in a company cloud. The owner, foremen and engineers use the Bustan app on a computer, phone or tablet, on the farm's Wi-Fi or, if the owner turns it on, from anywhere.

Backups can be copied to a USB stick in the server and, if the owner connects it, to a folder in the owner's own Google Drive. See the privacy policy for exactly what that involves.
