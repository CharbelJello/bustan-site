Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Bustan is farm management software for apple farms. This policy explains what data Bustan handles, where it is kept, and how the optional Google Drive backup works. "We" means the makers of Bustan; "the farm" means the farm that runs Bustan and its owner.

Where farm data lives

  • Each farm runs its own Bustan server (usually a Raspberry Pi at the farm). The farm's records are stored in that server's database: blocks, seasons, treatments, harvests, water turns, attendance and wages, sales, losses, and the names and phone numbers of the farm's foremen, workers, engineers and buyers.
  • App logins (usernames and passwords) are stored on the same server. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • We do not run a central database, and the farm's records are not copied to us.
  • If the farm agrees to support access in its contract, we can sign in to the farm's server to install updates, fix problems or restore a backup. We do this only to provide that support.

Using the app away from the farm

If the owner turns on remote access, the apps reach the farm's server through an encrypted tunnel provided by Tailscale (tailscale.com). The tunnel passes the app's requests to the farm's server; the records are still stored only on that server.

Google Drive backups (optional)

The owner can connect their own Google account so that the farm's backups are also copied to Google Drive. When they do:

  • Bustan asks Google for permission to see, create and change only the files Bustan itself creates in that Drive (Google's drive.file permission). Bustan cannot see, open or change any other file in the owner's Drive.
  • Bustan also receives the account's email address (Google's email and openid permissions), only to show the owner which account is connected.
  • Bustan uses this access for one purpose: uploading the farm's backup files to a "Bustan backups" folder in that Drive and deleting older Bustan backups from that folder, keeping as many days as the owner chose.
  • The access token that Google issues is stored only on the farm's own server. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Data received from Google is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with third parties, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • The owner can disconnect at any time in the app (Settings, Server, Backups, Disconnect Google Drive), which deletes the stored token and asks Google to revoke Bustan's access. Access can also be removed from the Google account's security settings (Third-party apps with account access). Backup files already in Drive stay there and belong to the owner.

Bustan's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Backups on a USB stick

If a USB stick is plugged into the farm's server, each backup is also copied to a "Bustan backups" folder on it. Nothing else on the stick is read or changed.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, tracking or analytics in the apps.
  • No selling or renting of any data.
  • No collection of the phone's location, contacts or photos. The phone apps use the camera only to scan a pairing QR code, and only when the user taps Scan.

Keeping and deleting data

The farm decides how long its records are kept, since they live on its own server. Backups are kept for the number of days the owner sets (14 by default) on the server, the USB stick and Google Drive. To delete everything, the owner can erase the server's storage and delete the "Bustan backups" folder from Drive and the USB stick.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
